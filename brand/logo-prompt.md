# TideEye — Logo generation brief (African identity)

Use this brief with an AI image generator (Midjourney, DALL·E, Ideogram, Gemini,
Firefly) or hand it to a designer. Generate on a **transparent or plain background**
so it drops into the app. Drop the final file at `brand/logo.png` (and ideally
`brand/logo.svg`) and it will replace the current mark.

---

## The concept

**TideEye = “the eye on the tide.”** A watchful eye, made of water, keeping African
communities safe. The mark should feel **African, trustworthy, and modern-tech at
once** — a guardian, not a gadget.

Core idea to combine into one clean symbol:
- an **eye** (watching, protection, early warning)
- whose **iris is a water drop / ripple** (freshwater, the thing we protect)
- with a subtle **satellite / signal** cue (an orbit ring, a scan line, or a
  concentric ripple that doubles as a radar sweep)

## Style

- **Modern flat vector**, geometric, clean lines, minimal. Not photorealistic.
- Confident and calm. Reads clearly at 24 px (app icon) and on a PDF header.
- One strong idea, not a busy illustration. Think Duolingo / Copernicus clarity,
  not a detailed crest.
- African identity expressed with **warmth and pattern**, not clichés: avoid
  wildlife safari tropes, avoid a literal map of Africa, avoid tribal-mask kitsch.
  Prefer **Adinkra-style geometry**, **Kente-inspired stripes as a small accent**,
  concentric water/river line-work, and warm earth tones alongside deep water blues.

## Colors (brand palette — keep to these)

- Deep water teal `#0E7490` (primary)
- Bright water cyan `#06B6D4` (secondary / the drop)
- Earth terracotta `#C2410C` (African warmth accent — use sparingly, e.g. an
  orbit dot or a single Kente stripe)
- Ink `#0F172A` (wordmark text)
- Works on both light `#F8FAFC` and a dark `#0B1024` background.

## Typography (for the wordmark)

- Wordmark: **“TideEye”** in **Lexend**, weight 700. Set “Tide” in ink `#0F172A`
  and “Eye” in deep water teal `#0E7490`.
- Optional tagline in Lexend 400, small caps or normal: **“The eye on the tide.”**

## Deliverables (please provide)

1. **Icon mark** only (the eye+drop symbol), square, transparent background.
2. **Horizontal lockup**: icon + “TideEye” wordmark side by side.
3. **Monochrome** version (single ink color) for stamps / the PDF footer.
4. A **dark-background** variant.

## Prompt (copy-paste starter)

> Minimal flat vector logo for “TideEye”, a satellite water-safety app for African
> communities. A stylised human eye whose iris is a water drop, wrapped by a thin
> concentric ripple that also reads as a radar/satellite sweep. Modern, geometric,
> clean, friendly and trustworthy. African identity shown through subtle
> Adinkra-style geometry and a single small Kente-inspired accent stripe — no
> wildlife, no map of Africa, no masks. Colors: deep teal #0E7490, water cyan
> #06B6D4, one small terracotta #C2410C accent. Flat, crisp edges, solid fills,
> no gradients on text, transparent background, high contrast, scalable to a tiny
> app icon. Wordmark “TideEye” in a clean geometric sans (Lexend), “Tide” in dark
> ink, “Eye” in teal.

## What to avoid
- Gradients on text (breaks in the PDF renderer).
- Photographic elements, drop shadows, 3D bevels.
- Cliché “Africa” imagery (continent silhouette, animals, spears, masks).
- More than 3–4 colors. Keep it legible at 24 px.
